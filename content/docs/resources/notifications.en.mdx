Each notification arrives with its actor and with `path`, where it
points. Working that out used to be the reader's job: eleven chained
reads, because a reply's address is the address of what it answers
plus an anchor. It is a join, and it happens where the joins are.

`path` comes without the language prefix — that belongs to the reader
— and comes back `null` when there is nowhere left to go.
