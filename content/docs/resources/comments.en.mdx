title: Comments
description: The replies under a post and under a profile.
full: true
---

In the database they are two tables, an accident of the order they
were built in. Here they are one resource: `on` says what the reply
sits under, and a reply's id is enough to change or remove it,
because both tables take their ids from the same generator.

The author decides who may reply, and the refusal is `403 forbidden`
with the reason in the message.
