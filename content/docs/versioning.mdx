---
title: Versioning
description: What can change under you, and what cannot.
---

The version is in the path: `/api/v1/...`.

**Additive changes ship into v1.** A new field on a response, a new optional
parameter, a new endpoint, a new scope. Write clients that ignore fields they
do not know, because new ones will appear.

**Anything that removes a field or changes its meaning waits for v2.** The two
would run side by side while v1 is deprecated, with a `Deprecation` header and
a sunset date announced before either means anything.

## What is not a contract

- `message` on an error. Match on `code`.
- The order of keys in an object.
- The exact wording of anything meant for a person to read.

## The website's own endpoints

There are two dozen route handlers under `/api` that are not this API. They are
the website's own, shaped around one screen each, and they change whenever that
screen changes. A bearer key is not accepted on any of them, so nothing can
come to depend on them by accident.
